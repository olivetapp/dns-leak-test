Privacy Policy of DNS Leak Test ◌

Olivet App provides DNS Leak Test ◌.

Declared data collection and use

  • Crash data for diagnostics
  • Device ID and advertising data for third-party advertising
  • Purchase history for entitlement management
  • Test results and saved internet providers are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. You can delete them in History and Settings, or by deleting the app. No account is needed.
  • The DNS resolver your device uses (your internet provider's or your VPN's) also sees the test lookups that pass through it.
  • The country of each address is found in a DB-IP table inside the app (IP Geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0); no network request is made for it. When Team Cymru gives no answer, the network owner of an address is also looked up in a second DB-IP table inside the app (IP to ASN Lite, CC BY 4.0), with no network request.

Declared external services

  • Akamai: during a test, DNS test names (whoami) go through your device's DNS resolver, and the app asks Akamai name servers directly for your public IP address. Akamai sees your public IP address (or your VPN's) and the address of your DNS server.
  • Google AdMob
  • Google: during a test, DNS test names (o-o.myaddr) go through your device's DNS resolver, and the app asks Google name servers directly for your public IP address. Google sees your public IP address (or your VPN's) and the address of your DNS server.
  • RevenueCat
  • Team Cymru: during a test, the app looks up which network owns each address through your device's DNS resolver. Team Cymru sees the addresses that are looked up and the address of your DNS server.

These declarations describe the approved app configuration.

How data is collected

The data listed above is collected automatically through the app and the external services named on this page while you use the app. It is used only for the purposes listed above.

Third-party services

Each external service named above receives only the data it needs for its stated purpose and provides the same or equal protection of user data as this policy and the App Store guidelines require.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only for as long as it is needed for the stated purposes, then deleted or anonymized. Deleting the app removes the data it stores on your device. To request deletion of other data, email the contact below; we will delete what we hold and pass the request to the external services.

Your choices

You can withdraw consent at any time through the privacy controls in the app, where available, or in your device settings, including Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. To revoke consent, email the contact below.
